GiveTrack Integrity Sentinel

Continuous automated financial anomaly engine safeguarding donor capital and audit integrity.

Automated Sentinel Verification Rules

  • Duplicate Transaction References: Flags identical bank/UPI transfer codes to prevent double-counting of donor capital.
  • Missing Bill References: Detects disbursements logged without invoice numbers or vendor receipts.
  • Overdrawn Campaign Balances: Identifies any campaign where verified expenditures exceed total raised contributions.
  • Reconciliation Imbalance: Verifies that Total Raised = Verified Spent + Remaining Reserve holds across all records.
  • Statistical Outlier Detection: Flags individual disbursements exceeding 2.5× the historical category average for administrative review.
